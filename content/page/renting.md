**目标：**

* 完成租房沟通流程，包括了解房源信息、价格协商、支付方式以及后续管理问题  
（Complete the house rental conversation, including understanding property details, negotiating rent, discussing payment methods, and handling post-rental management issues）

**挑战：**

1. 询问房屋类型、位置及基本信息  
   Ask about the type of property, location, and basic details.

2. 了解房屋装修风格、设施配置及居住条件  
   Ask about the decoration style, facilities, and living conditions.

3. 讨论租金价格、押金及租期，并尝试进行简单协商  
   Discuss rent, deposit, and lease duration, and attempt basic negotiation.

4. 确认支付方式及相关费用（如水电费、管理费等）  
   Confirm payment methods and additional costs (e.g., utilities, management fees).

5. 了解维修责任及物业管理支持  
   Ask about maintenance responsibilities and property management services.

6. 根据中介的问题做出自然回应（如预算、需求、偏好等）  
   Respond naturally to the agent’s questions about budget, needs, and preferences.

7. 在对话中表达接受、犹豫或提出进一步需求  
   Express agreement, hesitation, or additional requests during the conversation.
